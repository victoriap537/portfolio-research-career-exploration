# OpenAI — Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE), Healthcare, NYC

**[View the official OpenAI Careers posting](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-engineer-%28fde%29-healthcare-nyc-new-york-city/)**

## Role summary

This FDE owns technical deployments of AI systems inside healthcare organizations, including payers, providers, health systems, and healthcare technology companies. The role spans customer discovery through production rollout and handoff.

## Responsibilities in the posting

- Lead technical discovery and translate healthcare workflows into requirements.
- Design and implement production AI applications and agentic systems.
- Integrate with enterprise APIs, data platforms, electronic health records, claims systems, and operational tools.
- Define evaluations, validation evidence, human-review workflows, escalation paths, and launch criteria.
- Use error analysis, observability, and customer feedback to improve reliability and workflow impact.
- Turn deployment lessons into reusable architectures, integration patterns, and evaluation tools.

## Experience and domain knowledge listed

The posting asks for **6+ years** of relevant software engineering, ML/AI engineering, solutions engineering, technical consulting, or comparable experience. It also describes healthcare experience such as payer workflows, provider operations, EHR systems, or interoperability standards as valuable.

The role names technologies and practices including Epic, HL7, FHIR, privacy, security, authorization, governance, auditability, and HIPAA-related safeguards.

## What stands out

The engineer is accountable for more than a working prototype. The system must meet customer-specific acceptance criteria and operate within healthcare's privacy, security, and workflow constraints.

## Relevance to a student

This is an experienced full-time role, not an entry-level target. It is useful for seeing what deeper expertise can eventually involve: production engineering, integrations, evaluation, customer communication, and domain-specific reliability.
