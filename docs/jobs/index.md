# Job listing research

These are examples of how employers describe forward-deployed work. Requirements differ by company, customer domain, and seniority. A posting can close or change, so use the original employer page as the source of truth.

## Internship / early-career example

### [Google — Forward Deployed Engineering Intern, BS/MS, Summer 2027](/jobs/google-internship)

**Location:** Sunnyvale, New York, Reston, and other listed U.S. locations.

**Application timing:** The posting says to apply before **November 20, 2026**; applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Confirm the deadline and availability on the official page.

**Core requirements:** Current BS/MS student in a related technical field; Python or similar programming experience; experience taking production-grade AI solutions from concept to launch; cloud AI architecture; structured and unstructured data pipelines using vector databases and RAG-like architectures.

[Original Google posting](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/75665705386549958-forward-deployed-engineering-intern-bsms-summer-2027)

## Cross-posting observations

- Customer discovery and understanding real workflows appear repeatedly.
- Employers expect engineers to build and integrate working software, not only advise.
- Production readiness matters: testing, evaluation, reliability, security, and adoption are part of the work.
- Communication is technical work: engineers coordinate with customer teams and internal product or research groups.
- The exact stack varies by company and product. Python and JavaScript/TypeScript are useful foundations, but other roles use different languages and specialized tools.

## Important distinction

The Google internship is a direct student opportunity. The OpenAI full-time examples ask for several years of prior professional experience. They are useful for understanding the long-term role, but their seniority requirements should not be mistaken for typical internship requirements.
