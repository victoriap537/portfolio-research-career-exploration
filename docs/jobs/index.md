# Job listing research

The postings below are examples of how employers describe forward-deployed work. Requirements differ by company, customer domain, and seniority. A posting can close or change, so use the original employer page as the source of truth.

## Full-time roles

### OpenAI — Forward Deployed Engineer, Healthcare (New York City)

**Work described:** Own technical delivery from customer discovery and workflow scoping through architecture, implementation, evaluation, production deployment, adoption, and handoff. The role integrates AI applications with enterprise systems and healthcare workflows.

**Skills and experience named:** Production software or AI engineering; customer-facing technical ownership; enterprise APIs and data platforms; evaluations and reliability; privacy, security, authorization, auditability, and regulated-data practices. The specific posting asks for 6+ years of relevant experience and healthcare domain familiarity.

**What stands out:** This is not simply building a demo. The engineer is responsible for proving quality, meeting operational constraints, and helping a deployed system work inside a real organization.

[Original OpenAI posting](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-engineer-%28fde%29-healthcare-nyc-new-york-city/)

### OpenAI — Forward Deployed Software Engineer (New York City)

**Work described:** Build custom full-stack software with customers, use an iterative approach, scope prototypes and production deployments, code alongside customer engineering teams, and turn engagement lessons into reusable patterns.

**Skills and experience named:** 7+ years of professional full-stack engineering in the posting; relational databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL; customer-facing collaboration; practical software design and implementation.

**What stands out:** The FDSWE title is closely related to FDE, but this posting emphasizes full-stack product engineering and reusable software abstractions.

[Original OpenAI posting](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-software-engineer-nyc-new-york-city/)

### Palantir — Forward Deployed Engineer, Mixed Reality

**Work described:** Work on software used in complex operational settings, collaborating with technical and non-technical users as objectives evolve.

**Technologies named:** Java, Go, Rust, TypeScript, Android tooling, Unity, Gradle, CircleCI, and GitHub. The posting also values analytical problem-solving, data structures, storage systems, cloud infrastructure, and programming ability.

**What stands out:** FDE work is not limited to AI. The role can involve specialized products, devices, and software used directly by customers.

[Original Palantir posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/96a0ce26-cf84-4fa8-934b-acc4363620b2)

## Internship / early-career example

### Google — Forward Deployed Engineering Intern, BS/MS, Summer 2027

**Work described:** Develop complex AI applications, move prototypes toward production-grade agentic workflows, connect AI products to customer infrastructure and APIs, and build evaluation and observability pipelines.

**Skills implied by the responsibilities:** Software development; API and systems integration; understanding of data and infrastructure; testing and evaluation; reliability, safety, and latency awareness; collaboration with customer engineering teams.

**What stands out:** This is a direct internship-level example of forward deployed engineering. It shows that internship opportunities can involve real deployment-oriented engineering rather than only internal research.

[Original Google internship posting](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/75665705386549958-forward-deployed-engineering-intern-bsms-summer-2027)

## Cross-posting observations

- Customer discovery and understanding real workflows appear repeatedly.
- Employers expect engineers to build and integrate working software, not only advise.
- Production readiness matters: testing, evaluation, reliability, security, and adoption are part of the work.
- Communication is technical work: engineers coordinate with customer teams and internal product or research groups.
- The exact stack varies. Python and JavaScript/TypeScript appear in AI and full-stack contexts; other roles may use Java, Go, Rust, or domain-specific tools.
