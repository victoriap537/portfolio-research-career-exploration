# Palantir — Forward Deployed Engineer, Mixed Reality

*Employer posting reviewed November 17, 2026 · Full-time · Washington, D.C. · On-site*

[View the original Palantir job posting](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/96a0ce26-cf84-4fa8-934b-acc4363620b2)

## Role Summary

This role is part of Palantir's Mixed Reality Group, a team of researchers, 3D artists, designers, and engineers. They build mixed-reality applications for customers working in real-world environments.

## Main Responsibilities

- Work directly with customers to understand their needs.
- Build and test mixed-reality applications.
- Troubleshoot hardware and software problems.
- Develop custom applications for specific customers.
- Work with other engineers on technical projects.
- Help move projects from ideas to deployment.

## Technologies and Skills

The posting mentions Java, Go, Rust, and TypeScript, along with Android development tools, Unity, Gradle, CircleCI, and GitHub.

Other important skills include:
- Programming and software development.
- Problem-solving and debugging.
- Communication and teamwork.
- Working with technical and non-technical people.
- Adapting to changing customer needs.

## Requirements and Work Setting

The position is full-time and on-site in Washington, D.C. The posting prefers at least two years of relevant experience. Applicants must have an active U.S. security clearance or be eligible and willing to obtain one. Travel to customer sites may be required, up to 25%.

## What Stands Out

This role shows that forward-deployed engineering is not limited to generative AI or web applications. It can also involve hardware, mixed reality, and specialized software. Engineers work closely with customers to create solutions for real-world problems.

## Relevance to a Student

This is a full-time role, not an internship. Students can prepare by learning programming, practicing software development and debugging, working in teams, and building projects that solve practical problems.
