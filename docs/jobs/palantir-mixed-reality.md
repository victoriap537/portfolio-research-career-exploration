## Role summary

This role sits within Palantir's Mixed Reality Group. The posting describes a cross-disciplinary team of researchers, 3D artists, immersive UX designers, and engineers building applications for demanding real-world environments.

## Work described

- Collaborate with customers and operators to understand operational problems.
- Work with mixed-reality designers and developers.
- Deliver applications intended for use in real operational settings.
- Adapt to changing requirements and practical constraints.

## What stands out

This example shows that forward-deployed engineering is not limited to generative AI. The same broad pattern—working closely with users, understanding their environment, and delivering software for their needs—can apply to specialized products and hardware-connected settings.

## Relevance to a student

The posting is a full-time example rather than an internship pathway. It reinforces the importance of programming fundamentals, collaboration across disciplines, user-focused problem solving, and building software for the environment where it will actually be used.
