# OpenAI — Forward Deployed Engineer, NYC

<small>Employer posting reviewed November 12, 2026 · Full-time</small>

**[View the official OpenAI Careers posting](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-engineer-%28fde%29-nyc-new-york-city/)**

## Role summary

OpenAI describes FDEs as engineers who lead complex deployments of its models with customers. The work connects customer delivery with improvements to the company's core platform.

## Responsibilities in the posting

- Own technical delivery from an initial prototype through stable production.
- Build full-stack systems that solve customer problems.
- Work closely with customer teams to understand needs and guide adoption.
- Scope projects, sequence delivery, and remove blockers.
- Make trade-offs between scope, speed, and quality.
- Contribute code directly when needed.
- Turn repeated solutions into tools, playbooks, and reusable building blocks.
- Share field feedback with Research and Product teams.

## Experience listed

The posting asks for **5+ years of engineering or technical deployment experience**, including customer-facing work, and experience scoping and delivering complex systems in ambiguous environments.

## What stands out

The role is not only about implementing a specification. Engineers help define the problem, make delivery decisions, build the solution, and use what they learn from customers to improve reusable systems.

## Relevance to a student

This is an experienced full-time role. For preparation, focus on the foundations behind it: building complete software, integrating APIs, testing and deploying applications, debugging, and explaining technical decisions clearly.
