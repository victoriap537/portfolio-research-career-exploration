## Role summary

The intern works with Google's Cloud Consulting team as an embedded builder. The posting describes developing complex AI applications, moving prototypes toward production-grade agentic workflows, and connecting Google's AI products to customer infrastructure.

## Responsibilities in the posting

- Develop complex AI applications and move prototypes toward production-grade agentic workflows.
- Connect AI products to customer infrastructure, APIs, legacy data systems, and security boundaries.
- Build evaluation pipelines and observability frameworks to measure accuracy, safety, and latency.
- Identify repeated implementation patterns and turn them into reusable modules or product feedback.
- Work with customer engineering teams and encourage maintainable development practices.

## Minimum qualifications listed

- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical field.
- Experience with software development using Python or a similar language.
- Experience taking production-grade AI-driven solutions from conception to launch and architecting AI systems on cloud platforms such as Google Cloud.
- Experience building structured and unstructured data pipelines using vector databases and RAG-like architectures.

## Preferred qualifications listed

- Student in the penultimate academic year, with availability for a 12-week full-time internship.
- Experience with multi-agent systems and frameworks such as LangGraph, CrewAI, or Google's ADK.
- Experience leading technical discovery sessions.
- Familiarity with LLM metrics such as tokens per second and cost per request, plus tracing and state-management optimization.
