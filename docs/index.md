## Research question

**What do Forward Deployed Engineers do in practice, and what skills appear in real internship and full-time job descriptions?**

This site summarizes employer-published role descriptions. It separates what postings explicitly state from broader conclusions drawn across postings.

## Main finding

Forward Deployed Engineering combines hands-on software development with close work alongside customers. The engineer learns how a customer operates, turns that workflow into technical requirements, builds and integrates software, and helps get the solution working reliably in production.

The job title is not standardized. Similar work may appear under titles such as Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Deployment Engineer, Solutions Engineer, or Customer Engineer. The actual responsibilities matter more than the title.

## What this research compares

- **An internship pathway:** Google's Summer 2027 Forward Deployed Engineering internship.
- **Experienced engineering roles:** Current OpenAI FDE and Forward Deployed Software Engineer postings.
- **A different product environment:** Palantir's Forward Deployed Engineer, Mixed Reality role.
